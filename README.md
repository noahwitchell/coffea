# Coffea Arabica

A two-player pocket strategy game. Plant coffee species on a 4×4 mountainside and be the first to grow the ultimate hybrid: all four species in a row, a column or a 2×2 square. Your rival can finish what you start.

Play: https://noahwitchell.github.io/coffea/ (on iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).

No build step. `python3 -m http.server` and open it. Rules tests: `node --test tests/model.test.mjs`.
