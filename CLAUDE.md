# Coffea Arabica — notes for Claude

- Owner: Noah. His board game (first draft "Arabica Rulebook v1.0", ~/Downloads/ArabicaRules_v100.pages) as a phone PWA. Look: flat and modern (Monument Valley / NYT Games), no gradients, blur or glossy shading. Paper background, ink UI, DM Serif Display headings. Board = flat clay terraces with risers under a pale sky and flat mountains, rows numbered 1 (bottom) to 4 (top). Pieces = Noah's six-lobed coffee shrubs with 4 cherries, drawn flat (solid top + solid side face, no shading); he asked to keep that shape. Species colours are all greens (his call), cherries always red: Arabica chartreuse, Racemosa grass, Robusta pale mint (Noah asked for it paler), Liberica deep teal — keep them apart by lightness as well as hue. Noah's 3D board render (bubbly bushes, Lobster script) was only a starting idea and was rejected as too bubbly.
- No build step, no frameworks, no npm deps. ES modules served statically, same setup as ~/Claude/EveryDay and ~/Claude/FretFlow.
- All rules live in `js/model.js` (row 0 in code = top = "row 4" on screen). Run `node --test tests/model.test.mjs` after changing them. Computer opponent in `js/ai.js` (easy random / normal = don't hand over a win / hard = one full turn of expectimax).
- Rulings the rulebook left open (shown in the app's rules sheet): disease is orthogonal and checked before the win; transplants obey altitude; a piece with no legal spot goes back and the turn ends; 8 straight passes = draw.
- Monoculture disease animation (blight() in app.js): shiver, then one smooth ~1s crossfade from the full-colour shrub into the withered symbol (art.js WITHERED), then it fades off while leaves drop. Noah asked for a dying colour change, but smooth, not stepped shades of brown.
- Steady interface: controls hold their size and position; disable, don't remove.
- Game in progress saved in localStorage `coffea.v1`.
- Icon = one Racemosa (medium green) shrub, large (≈48px top/bottom margin at 512), on the four striped terrace bands (same colours + risers as the board) — Noah's pick after trying Arabica and deep teal. Rebuild: `node tools/make_icon.mjs && zsh tools/render_icons.sh`.
- Public repo github.com/noahwitchell/coffea, GitHub Pages from main (push = deploy). Bump CACHE in sw.js when shipping.
